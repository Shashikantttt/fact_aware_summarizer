# Indian Standard Demo Samples for Teacher Evaluation

This directory contains curated, authentic evaluation texts specifically formatted for demonstration to teachers, evaluators, and project reviewers.

## Files Included:

1. `research_paper_indic_nlp.txt`
   - **Type**: Full Academic Research Paper
   - **Domain**: Multilingual NLP for Low-Resource Indian Languages (IIT Madras / Bhashini Context)
   - **Features**: Includes standard academic metadata (Affiliations, Abstract, Roman numeral section headers I-IV, IEEE style in-text citations [1, 2], and a formal References bibliography).
   - **Purpose**: Demonstrates the Academic Paper Cleaner (strips citations, DOIs, and bibliography), isolates the author Abstract as a gold-standard benchmark, and evaluates TF-IDF against BART with NLI claim verification.

2. `news_isro_chandrayaan.txt`
   - **Type**: Indian Journalistic News Article (The Hindu / PTI Standard)
   - **Domain**: Space Technology & ISRO Lunar Mission
   - **Features**: Dense factual reporting with verifiable dates, figures, launch locations (Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota), and payload specifications.
   - **Purpose**: Demonstrates high-precision factual consistency checking and evidence grounding on Indian technological achievements.

3. `news_upi_digital_infrastructure.txt`
   - **Type**: Indian Financial / Economic News Article (Economic Times / LiveMint Standard)
   - **Domain**: Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) & Fintech
   - **Features**: Economic statistics, RBI regulatory guidelines, NPCI volume metrics, and bilateral cross-border linkage details (Singapore PayNow, UAE).
   - **Purpose**: Demonstrates factual verification on financial numbers, policy claims, and organizational entities.
